#design #root
Sublimation is a 1.20.1 Expert pack based around tech automation and brutal survival.
## Design Principles
### Survival Challenges should never truly become "just an annoyance"

### Water is a resource

### Automation challenges shouldn't just have "one solution"

### "Magic Gray Boxes" should not be the absolute meta

### Enemies should serve as a challenge while not detracting from the experience

### Voiding shouldn't be the go-to solution to all byproducts
The player should be encouraged to process byproducts created through their various processes rather than throw them in a (non-existent) trash can.
This is done through a few design choices:
- Common byproducts among recipes to encourage building larger processing facilities that take input from multiple setups.
- No easy voiding blocks/upgrades.
- Most of such byproducts should be done through fluids as items are easy to void even without dedicated trash blocks, item byproducts should be simpler to process and have such processing be easy to fit into pre-existing automations to encourage not just throwing them into a cactus.
